## About Me

My name is Joe, and I'm a Lead Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer with 6+ years of experience consulting for Microsoft and other Fortune 500 companies.

I've led international teams in AI solution design & delivery for over 20 clients, with projects spanning multiple domains, including:

- deep reinforcement learning for manufacturing optimization,
- operations research (algorithm design & implementation for fast food order sequencing),
- fraud detection for a major Canadian credit card issuer,
- customer analytics (e.g. customer churn, customer lifetime value),
- explainable machine learning, and
- numerical modeling & simulation.

I have a Ph.D. in Physics from Washington State University.  My graduate research involved building numerical simulations of novel fiber optic devices with Python, an experience which turned out to be unexpectedly useful for data science applications, including building discrete event and physics-based simulations for deep reinforcement learning projects (see below).

## Open Source Projects

I built several projects for Microsoft to showcase their deep reinforcement learning platform *Bonsai*.

- *Plastic extrusion simulation for manufacturing optimization* ([GitHub](https://github.com/microsoft/microsoft-bonsai-api/tree/main/Python/samples/plastic-extrusion)).

- *Hospital bed utilization simulation* ([GitHub](https://github.com/NealAnalyticsLLC/bonsai_simpy_demo)) to demonstrate the integration of *Bonsai* with *SimPy*, a popular lightweight platform for discrete event simulation in Python.
